# Precommitted kill and change criteria

Review after the first 20 completed behavior-first interviews in a consistent segment, then every 10 interviews. Do not move thresholds after seeing results without documenting why.

| Criterion | Threshold | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Pain prevalence | Fewer than 20% of interviewed in-scope prospects score HIGH/EXTREME with at least one observed behavior or quantified claim | Kill the segment or problem thesis. |
| Multi-provider need | More than 70% prefer one provider and report no trigger to add another within 12 months | Stop targeting that segment. |
| Willingness to test | Fewer than 3 of 20 commit named technical time to a defined sandbox test | Reposition or kill; compliments do not count. |
| Path to payment | No qualified company identifies a buyer/budget and conditional payment path after 20 interviews | Do not build production adapters; test a different buyer/value proposition. |
| Pilot conversion | Fewer than 2 of 5 qualified design partners accept a paid or explicitly conditional paid pilot | Rework offer/pricing once; then kill or pivot if repeated. |
| Second-provider value | Controlled second adapter reduces neither implementation effort nor operational steps by at least 30% against direct baseline | Redesign the abstraction or abandon “one integration” positioning. |
| Reconciliation value | Qualified teams do not rank reconciliation among top-three pains and will not measure it | Remove it as the wedge; do not build a large subsystem. |
| Provider permission | Required provider contracts prohibit aggregation, routing, data use, or customer model | Change architecture/contract path or stop that provider strategy. |
| Regulatory economics | Legal path requires capital, liability, controls, or licensing that makes validated pricing contribution-negative | Change funds flow/business model or stop. |
| Acquisition | Fully loaded CAC payback remains above 24 months after two tested repeatable channels and adequate cohorts | Change segment/channel/economics; do not scale spend. |
| Safety | Critical tenant, settlement, reconciliation, or security controls remain unresolved at pilot gate | No real-money production, regardless of demand. |

Outcomes:

- **DOUBLE DOWN:** repeated HIGH/EXTREME behavior evidence, committed tests, payment path, positive measured pilot outcome.
- **REPOSITION:** pain exists but buyer, workflow, message, or pricing is wrong.
- **REBUILD NECESSARY LAYER:** demand and payment exist but provider/ledger/reconciliation architecture fails the experiment.
- **KILL/PIVOT:** customers do not care, will not commit time/pay, or regulatory/provider economics are structurally unattractive.
