# Design partner program — sandbox only

## Offer

Ricarut helps selected teams test and design one multi-provider payout workflow using a unified sandbox, simulated failures, a draft provider contract, and ledger/reconciliation experiments.

The partner receives early sandbox access, direct founder support, technical workflow review, structured failure simulation, and a written findings summary. Ricarut receives workflow evidence, anonymized requirements, failure cases, usability feedback, pricing feedback, and permission to measure agreed outcomes.

This offer does not include real-money movement, production financial services, regulatory coverage, uptime guarantees, a live provider connection, or a promise that a requested feature will be built.

## Qualification gate

All must be evidenced:

- Problem exists today.
- Company directly experiences it.
- Cost is measurable through time, money, incidents, or manual steps.
- Team will complete a defined sandbox test.
- Technical participant will provide feedback and artifacts where lawful.
- A realistic budget owner and path to payment exist if success criteria are met.

Friendly interest, a signup, a demo attendance, an internal seed account, or “nice project” does not qualify.

## Engagement

1. Baseline interview and artifact review.
2. Written hypothesis and success criteria.
3. Two-week sandbox workflow test.
4. Weekly technical session, capped at an agreed support level.
5. Outcome measurement and pricing conversation.
6. Decision: stop, extend as a paid technical pilot, or record why it failed.

Publication is opt-in and separate. Default permission is internal-only.
